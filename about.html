About Me.

Origin

I grew up in Jakarta and studied Visual Communication Design at Universitas Multimedia Nusantara, majoring in Interactive Media Design. Even early on, I was drawn to things that felt alive, where visuals and logic worked together to create something interactive.

Early System

For my final thesis, I created an Android endless runner game inspired by Alto’s Adventure. I was fascinated by how that game made visual beauty and gameplay feel inseparable, and I wanted to explore that balance in something of my own.

Early Signal

My first UX internship at Bridestory in late 2018 was the real beginning. It taught me almost everything I know about product design in practice, especially the parts college never really prepares you for.

Current State

After that, I joined DANA and have been there ever since, growing from a junior role into Lead UI UX Designer. The biggest shift in my thinking has been understanding how strongly business decisions shape the user experience of an app.

Design View

That perspective changed how I design. I think a good product is not just visually polished or easy to use, it also needs to make sense within the goals, constraints, and realities behind it.
